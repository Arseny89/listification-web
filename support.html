Support

Listification  ·  Frequently asked questions

Need a hand? Email support@listification.app and include your device model and iOS version. We usually reply within a couple of days.

How do I sync my lists across devices?

Listification uses your private iCloud account to sync. On each device:

  • Sign in to iCloud (Settings → your name).
  • Make sure iCloud Drive is turned on.
  • Keep Listification enabled in the iCloud app list.
  • Use the same Apple ID on every device.

Syncing happens automatically in the background over Wi-Fi or cellular. The first sync after installing can take a few minutes.

My lists aren't syncing. What can I try?

  • Check that the device has a working internet connection.
  • Confirm the same Apple ID is signed in on both devices.
  • Open the app and leave it in the foreground for a minute or two.
  • Restart the app, or restart the device.
  • Check that your iCloud storage isn't full (Settings → your name → iCloud).

I bought "Unlimited Lists" but it's not active on my other device.

Open the app on that device and choose Restore Purchases on the unlock screen. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID, so make sure you're signed in to the App Store with the same account you used to buy.

How do I delete my data?

  • Individual items: swipe or use edit mode to delete lists, folders, or entries.
  • Everything on one device: delete the app.
  • iCloud copy: Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Listification → Delete.

Listification does not collect any personal data on a server, so there is nothing for us to delete on your behalf. See the Privacy Policy for details.

Does Listification work offline?

Yes. All your lists are stored on the device and are fully available without a connection. Changes sync to iCloud the next time you're online.

I have a feature request or found a bug.

We'd love to hear it — email support@listification.app. Screenshots and steps to reproduce help a lot.
