Privacy Policy

Listification  ·  Last updated: September 10, 2026

In short:
  • Listification does not collect, sell, or share your personal data.
  • Your lists live on your device. If you enable iCloud, they sync through your own private Apple iCloud account — the developer cannot see them.
  • There are no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking SDKs.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple. The developer never receives your payment details.

This Privacy Policy explains how the Listification app ("the App", "we", "us") handles information. The App is developed by Arsenii Kornienko ("the Developer"). By using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. The App has no user accounts, no sign-up, and no login. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, or location.

The content you create in the App — lists, list items, folders, and related settings — is stored locally on your device using Apple's on-device storage frameworks. This content is not transmitted to the Developer or to any third-party server operated by the Developer.

2. iCloud Sync

The App optionally uses Apple iCloud (CloudKit private database) to sync your lists across devices signed in to the same Apple ID and to help your data survive reinstalling the App.

  • This data is stored in your own private iCloud account, managed by Apple.
  • The Developer has no access to the contents of your iCloud private database.
  • Sync is handled by the operating system. You can disable it in the iOS Settings app under your name → iCloud, or by signing out of iCloud.
  • Apple's handling of iCloud data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

3. Push Notifications

The App may use silent background notifications solely to keep your iCloud data in sync across your devices. These notifications are a technical part of iCloud sync and are not used for marketing or advertising.

4. Purchases

The App offers an optional one-time in-app purchase ("Unlimited Lists"). All purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. The Developer does not receive or store your credit card number, billing address, or other payment information. Apple provides only anonymous confirmation that a purchase was completed. Apple's processing of purchase data is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

5. Ratings Prompt

The App may occasionally ask you to rate it using Apple's standard system prompt. Any rating or review you choose to leave is handled entirely by Apple and is not linked to you by the Developer.

6. Analytics and Tracking

The App contains no analytics software, no advertising, and no third-party tracking. We do not use tracking technologies as defined by Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework, and the App does not request permission to track you.

Apple may provide the Developer with aggregate, anonymous App Store statistics (such as total downloads) if you have opted in to sharing analytics with developers in your device settings. This information is not personally identifiable and is controlled by Apple.

7. Local Settings

The App stores preferences (such as appearance and feature settings) locally on your device using the system settings store. These preferences remain on your device and are not sent to the Developer.

8. Children's Privacy

The App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. The App is safe for users of all ages in this respect.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

  • On-device data: deleting a list, folder, or item removes it from the App. Deleting the App removes its local data from that device.
  • iCloud data: you control this through your Apple ID. You can delete synced content from within the App, or manage and remove App data in the iOS Settings app under your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.
  • Because the Developer holds no personal data, there is nothing for the Developer to delete on your behalf.

10. Third Parties

The only third party involved in the App's core functionality is Apple, which provides device storage, iCloud, push delivery, and payment processing. The App does not integrate advertising networks, social media SDKs, or external analytics providers.

11. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under privacy laws such as the GDPR or the CCPA, including the right to access, correct, or delete personal data held about you. Because the Developer does not collect or store any personal data, these requests are effectively satisfied by your own control over the data on your device and in your iCloud account. If you have questions, contact us using the details below.

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after an update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
